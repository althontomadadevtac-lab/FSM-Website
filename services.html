Services

Our service model covers the essential activities needed to keep customer assets organized, maintained, and properly documented.

01

Asset Registration

Create structured records for customer equipment, locations, serial details, categories, and service-relevant information.

02

Preventive Maintenance

Define recurring service schedules and routine maintenance activities based on agreed intervals.

03

Inspection Services

Perform scheduled or on-demand inspections with clear findings and recommended follow-up actions.

04

Corrective Maintenance

Coordinate repair work for identified faults, breakdowns, and service exceptions.

05

Field Service Coordination

Assign work to technicians, track progress, and document the result of on-site service activities.

06

Service Reporting

Maintain a usable record of work performed, parts used, findings, and service completion status.
